Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 24, 2026

1. Overview

Elvate ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our mobile application and website, how it is used, and how your privacy is safeguarded.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data footprint. We do not require mandatory registration, phone numbers, or credit card details to use Elvate.

  • Guest & Device Identity: A random, anonymous identifier is generated on your device to maintain your learning streak, XP progress, and completed classes locally.
  • Audio & Voice Interaction: When you practice speaking with Miss Nova, audio input is captured temporarily in-memory on your device solely to evaluate pronunciation and provide immediate feedback. Raw audio files are not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Telemetry: Anonymized application crash logs and lesson completion counters to detect performance issues and improve learning reliability.

3. Data Security & Storage

All communication between your device and our cloud infrastructure is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Database access is protected by strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring that only authorized services can process account telemetry.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or voice data with any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or commercial marketing platforms. Never.

5. Push Notifications

If you opt in to receive practice reminders, an anonymous notification token is stored to deliver daily speaking streak reminders. You can disable notifications at any time in your Android system settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Elvate is designed for general audiences and contains no offensive, monetized, or predatory mechanics. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, you can reach our team directly at support@elvate.app.
